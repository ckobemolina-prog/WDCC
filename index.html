Common questions

Rental Services FAQs

General guidance for planning your next step. Specific rental and insurance terms must be confirmed directly with the appropriate provider.

Can I get a replacement vehicle while my car is being repaired?

Replacement transportation may be available depending on provider inventory, driver eligibility, rental terms, and any applicable insurance coverage. Contact the rental provider and your insurer to confirm your options.

Will insurance cover the rental cost?

Rental coverage depends on your policy, claim circumstances, limits, and insurer approval. Review your coverage directly with your insurance company before making a rental commitment.

What information should I have ready?

Have a safe contact number, your preferred dates, vehicle needs, and any non-sensitive claim reference information available. Avoid submitting full policy documents or sensitive personal details through this staging website.

Does submitting this form confirm a booking or guarantee?

No. This is an inquiry form concept, not a booking system. Availability, pricing, coverage, guarantees, and rental agreements are not confirmed until authorized providers communicate their terms directly.
