Insurance Claims & Collision Repair FAQs

General guidance to help prepare for a conversation. Claim outcomes, coverage, repair approvals, estimates, and timelines must be confirmed with the appropriate authorized party.

Who decides whether an insurance claim is covered?

Your insurance company evaluates coverage under your policy and the circumstances of the claim. Contact your insurer or claims adjuster for the official decision and details about any deductible or limits.

Can this page approve my repair or estimate?

No. This page is an information and routing concept only. Estimates, approvals, repairs, and scheduling must be confirmed by the authorized collision repair facility and other relevant parties.

What information should I prepare for a claim question?

Have your insurer’s name, a non-sensitive claim reference if appropriate, the date of the incident, and a concise question ready. Do not submit full policy documents or sensitive identifying details through this staging form.

Does submitting this form guarantee a claim outcome?

No. Submitting an inquiry does not determine coverage, approve repairs, establish liability, guarantee reimbursement, or create a contractual commitment. Obtain written confirmation from the relevant authorized party.
